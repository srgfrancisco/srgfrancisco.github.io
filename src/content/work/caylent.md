Caylent is an AWS Premier Consulting Partner. I was on the Service Delivery team
as a Cloud Architect and delivered six clients. Consulting work has a particular
shape: you inherit someone else's constraints, you have weeks rather than
quarters, and the handover matters as much as the build.

## Migration and modernisation

**EVgo** — a lift-and-reshape migration of critical workloads from Rackspace to
**AWS EKS**.

**AoPS** — a container-based platform on ECS Fargate, CircleCI and GitHub.

## Network and multi-account governance

**TeleTracking** — a **hub-and-spoke** architecture across three regions in the
US and Europe, centralising ingress and egress with Transit Gateway, WAF and
load balancers.

**Web3 Pro** — **AWS Control Tower for Terraform**, automating account creation
and customisation under strict security and governance policies.

## Data and ML

**Whatnot** — training and serving pipelines on **SageMaker**, migrating the
recommendation engine off SpellML and extending their MLOps practice.

**PlanetDDS** — adopting **Datadog** as cloud-agnostic centralised logging, a
single pane of glass across AWS, GCP, Azure and on-premises.

## The architect part

Beyond the builds: discovery with clients, converting a SOW into an actual
project plan, aligning with Engagement and Delivery Managers, and mentoring
engineers on delivery teams.
